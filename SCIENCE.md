## Positioning choices

The website emphasizes signals that can be difficult to resolve with conventional Raman and that add information beyond routine culture parameters. It does not state that other analytical technologies cannot detect these metabolites. LC–MS remains a complementary established method.

SERS enhancement depends on proximity to a suitable surface. Detection, specificity and reproducibility depend on substrate, analyte, concentration, adsorption and sample matrix. The page therefore avoids unsupported numerical detection limits, simultaneous analyte counts, turnaround times, costs, commercial availability or system integration claims.

Prospect Bioprocess and Prospect Health are presented as development and research directions. A commercial or diagnostic positioning change should be supported by application-specific evidence.

Only the three papers above appear as scientific references on the site. General background papers without Javier Plou as an author were excluded as requested.
