01 / The signals that matter

There is more to cell state
than glucose and lactate.

Stress can change the molecules cells release into their surroundings. These extracellular metabolites offer a complementary view of what is happening inside.

OUR FOCUS

Small molecules.
Meaningful changes.

Purine metabolites can reveal shifts in cell metabolism and stress responses. Their interpretation depends on the biological model and experimental conditions.

Explore the published research
01 Hypoxanthine+
PURINE METABOLISM & CELLULAR STRESS

Extracellular hypoxanthine accumulation was observed during stress-induced cell death in the tumor models studied. SERS made these changes accessible through their spectral signatures.

Plou et al. · 2020
02 Adenine & related purines+
A BROADER METABOLIC PICTURE

Adenine-related and hypoxanthine-related signals help investigate changes in extracellular purine profiles. They are part of a molecular pattern, rather than a universal readout of stress.

Plou et al. · 2020
03 Methylthioadenosine (MTA)+
METABOLIC COMMUNICATION

In MTAP-deficient cancer models, SERS tracked secreted MTA and its metabolism by fibroblasts. This extends the perspective from cellular stress to communication between cells.

Valera, Plou et al. · 2023

03 / Why SERS

Weak Raman signals.
Amplified possibilities.

Surface-enhanced Raman scattering uses nanostructured metal surfaces to enhance the optical fingerprints of nearby molecules. It opens a route to sensitive, label-free metabolic analysis.

01

Reveal weak signals

Enhancement at the sensing surface helps detect metabolites that can be challenging to resolve with conventional Raman.

02

Read molecular patterns

Investigate several spectral features together to capture changes in the extracellular metabolic profile.

03

Move toward simpler workflows

Published work combines SERS with microfluidics and computational analysis to streamline secretome screening.

SERS complements established analytical methods, including LC–MS. Sensitivity, selectivity and reproducibility depend on the molecule, substrate and sample matrix.
