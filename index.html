The platform

From sensor
to decision.

Four pieces we design and build ourselves, engineered to work as one system, so every drop becomes a reliable metabolic fingerprint.

01

SERS sensors

Nanoplasmonic chips made in-house that amplify the Raman signal of metabolites in ~1 µL of supernatant or plasma, with no sample preparation.

36 zones28 × 28 mmSingle use
02

Optical reader

Our own reader, designed around our sensors. A high-throughput benchtop unit today; next, a module that integrates directly into the bioreactor.

<1 min / readBenchtop → in-line
03

Software

Spectral processing (baseline, cosmic rays, normalisation, MCR-ALS deconvolution) and machine-learning models that turn a spectrum into metabolites and process alerts.

ML modelsCloud
04

Spectral library

Our proprietary database of SERS spectra from cell cultures and CAR-T patients. Every new measurement grows it and sharpens the models.

>5,000 spectra>20 reference metabolites

Fast, label-free metabolic readouts to understand and optimise cultures while a process is being developed.

Manufacturing

  • Process alerts from metabolic shifts
